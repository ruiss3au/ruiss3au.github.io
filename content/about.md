## LLM usage

Article badges describe how large language models (LLMs) contributed to producing
an article. This is the site's labeling scheme, not a declaration of compliance
with external disclosure guidelines.

- LLM-generated: substantial article prose was written by a model. This label
  also applies when models assisted with other tasks alongside writing.
- LLM-assisted: the prose was human-written, with model help for research,
  analysis, editing, or proofreading.
- No LLM used: the author explicitly declares that no model was involved in
  producing the article.
- LLM usage unspecified: the available metadata does not establish the model's
  role. Older articles carried a generic badge by default, so that badge alone
  does not establish whether or how a model was used.

These labels do not identify model names or versions, or establish whether an
article's sources and claims have been independently checked.
