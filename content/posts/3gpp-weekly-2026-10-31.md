Radio Access Network Working Group 2 (RAN2) has asked RAN4 to check its understanding that a device can send Layer 1 measurement reports for a configured candidate secondary cell even when that cell is deactivated. The request concerns secondary-cell activation during Layer 1/Layer 2 Triggered Mobility (LTM), under the Release 20 mobility enhancements Phase 5 work item. [RP-260232](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/tsg_ran/TSG_RAN/TSGR_111/Docs/RP-260232.zip) [R4-2630011](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/tsg_ran/WG4_Radio/TSGR4_121/Docs/R4-2630011.zip)

## Deactivation should not prevent candidate-cell reports

RAN2's stated understanding is that user equipment (UE) can report measurements for LTM candidate secondary cells according to the LTM configuration, regardless of whether a candidate is currently a deactivated secondary cell. It asks RAN4 whether that understanding is acceptable and requests feedback for future work. This is a request for a cross-group check, rather than evidence of RAN4 agreement. [R4-2630011](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/tsg_ran/WG4_Radio/TSGR4_121/Docs/R4-2630011.zip)

## Reusing Release 18 and 19 capabilities

A separate RAN2 liaison asks RAN1 and RAN4 to confirm reuse of relevant Release 18 and Release 19 LTM device capabilities. RAN2 says the candidate-cell scope under those capabilities extends to both candidate special cells and candidate secondary cells. It explicitly identifies that extension as a reason to align with the other groups on reuse. [R4-2630009](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/tsg_ran/WG4_Radio/TSGR4_121/Docs/R4-2630009.zip)

RAN2 also says some other capabilities remain under discussion and will be finalized at future meetings. The confirmation request therefore leaves a specific issue open: whether RAN1 and RAN4 have concerns about applying the earlier capabilities to that expanded candidate-cell scope. [R4-2630009](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/tsg_ran/WG4_Radio/TSGR4_121/Docs/R4-2630009.zip)

## October requests, not November outcomes

Both liaisons originate from RAN2's Jeju meeting on 12-16 October 2026 and appear as incoming documents for RAN4's Calgary meeting on 16-20 November. They record RAN2's requests, not outcomes from the November meeting. [R4-2630011](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/tsg_ran/WG4_Radio/TSGR4_121/Docs/R4-2630011.zip) [R4-2630009](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/tsg_ran/WG4_Radio/TSGR4_121/Docs/R4-2630009.zip)
